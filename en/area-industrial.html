Industrial Infrastructures and Operations with Efficiency and Safety

H&S supports industrial growth through integrated solutions, ranging from the design and construction of factory units to maintenance, automation and operational management. Our focus is to ensure productivity, safety, compliance and sustainability, in alignment with the best international practices.

What we do

  • • EPC projects (Engineering, Procurement and Construction) for industrial units and plants
  • • Electromechanical maintenance, utilities and facilities
  • • Automation, instrumentation and monitoring (SCADA/IoT)
  • • Metal structure workshop
  • • Lifting and assembly solutions for metal structures

Sectors served

01. Agri-food & Beverages

Implementation and optimization of processing lines, industrial refrigeration systems and utilities (steam, compressed air and chilled water), ensuring efficiency, reliability and hygienic-sanitary control.

02. Construction Materials

Development of prefabricated plants, cements and ceramics, with automation, continuous monitoring and real-time quality control, ensuring productive consistency and waste reduction.

03. Oil & Gas / Energy

Industrial projects and auxiliary infrastructures, focusing on process safety, technical inspections, mechanical integrity and operational continuity, in compliance with international sector standards.

H&S Differentiators

  • Compliance and applicable certifications (ISO, OSHA)
  • Integrated engineering: civil, electrical, mechanical and automation
  • Predictive maintenance plans and OEE indicators
  • Sustainable projects, with reduced consumption and waste

How we work

  1. Diagnosis and Survey — Technical analysis, risk assessment and feasibility study.
  2. Project and Planning — Detailed engineering, resource definition and realistic schedules.
  3. Execution and Commissioning — Assembly, testing, validation and assisted system startup.
  4. Assisted Operation — Team training, SOP issuance and implementation of maintenance routines.
