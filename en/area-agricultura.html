Agriculture & Fishing

Home Agriculture & Fishing

Cultivating the Future, Enhancing Opportunities

H&S actively invests in the primary sector, developing structured projects in agriculture and fishing, focused on food self-sufficiency, community subsistence and Angola's economic growth.

Nursery and Landscaping

With nurseries located in Camama (5 hectares) and Quenguela (30 hectares), we provide plants, ornamental species and complete landscaping solutions for large-scale projects, including:

  • Kuditemo Village Landscaping
  • EPAL Headquarters - Talatona
  • Viana Park
  • Igreja Simão Toco
  • Camama Acacias

Farm

Located between Camama and Caxito, our farm constitutes a hub for agricultural production and livestock breeding, contributing to rural development, food security and supply to the national market.

Capesca Project

H&S is developing a large-scale private fishing project, covering the entire value chain — from fishing and mariculture to storage, processing and export of seafood products.

The Capesca Project includes:
  • Terrestrial aquaculture ponds
  • Aquaculture products processing factory
  • Ice factory
  • Small vessel factory (small shipyard)

Project Impact:

01.
Job Creation

Creation of jobs, prioritizing young Angolans.

02.
Domestic Market

Contribute to reducing fishing product prices for the end consumer, strengthening internal supply.

03.
Export

Boost the export of Angolan products, promoting the country in international markets.
