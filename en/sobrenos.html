For nearly 20 years, we have been driving structural projects that promote urban development, generate employment and create sustainable value for Angola.

To design, build and operate infrastructures, housing and industrial solutions that drive sustainable development, improve quality of life, generate employment and strengthen strategic cooperation between Angola and China.
