About us

19 Years Building Angola

Grupo H&S is a multinational company with a consolidated presence in Angola, operating in the sectors of Civil Construction and Infrastructure, Real Estate Developments, Industry, Agriculture and Fishing, and Investments. We connect capital, technology and human talent to drive sustainable and innovative development in the country.

With a multicultural team and strategic international partnerships, Grupo H&S transforms ideas into projects of excellence — from residential condominiums and commercial spaces to large-scale industrial solutions — promoting economic growth, job creation and social progress in Angola.

2006 — Arrival in Angola

Beginning of the first civil construction works and establishment of Grupo H&S presence in the country.

2007–2012 — Consolidation in works and housing

Execution of housing and infrastructure projects in Luanda and provinces, consolidating the brand in the sector.

2013–2018 — Real estate expansion

First private condominiums and own portfolio: Pérola Imperial (Morro Bento), Bela Vista (Benfica – classic-modern style seaside), Kutolola (next to Xyami Nova Vida) and development of Austin Residential.

2019–2021 — Social Responsibility

Agreement for the project "Born Free to Shine" with the First Lady's Office (Nov 29, 2019), OAFLAD/UA initiative — focus on maternal and child health.

2023 — Shopping Popular & investments

Inauguration of the 1st phase of Shopping Popular (Luanda) and announcement of investments exceeding 1 B USD in the country.

2024–2025 — New developments

Consolidation of strategic projects: Camama Park, CTA – Automotive Technology Center, IB Industrial Pole, Jardim de belas etc ..
