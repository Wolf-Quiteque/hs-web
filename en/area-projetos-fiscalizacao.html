CIAE — Integrated Business Support Centre

The CIAE – Integrated Business Support Centre was created with the purpose of facilitating, simplifying and accelerating business sector development, bringing together in one place essential services for entrepreneurs and investors. This centre was created to offer integrated and efficient service, allowing companies to have quick access to the main services needed for the creation, legalisation, management and growth of their businesses.

Total forecast of 1,000 jobs. The CIAE will include the presence of important institutional entities, including the Service for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (SME), AIPEX, the General Tax Administration (AGT), Commercial Banks, the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights of Angola and the Visa and Border Services (VFS).

01.
Company Incorporation and Formalisation

Complete support in the process of creating and legally registering companies in a single location.

02.
Private Investment Support

AIPEX services for the promotion and facilitation of national and international private investment.

03.
Tax and Revenue Services

General Tax Administration (AGT) services for fiscal and tax regularisation.

04.
Account Opening and Access to Financing

Presence of commercial banks for opening business accounts and access to credit lines.

05.
Legal and Administrative Documentation

Services from the Ministry of Justice and Visa and Border Services (VFS) for all necessary documentation.

More than a service space, the CIAE aims to be a true engine of economic development, stimulating entrepreneurship, attracting investment and strengthening the business environment in Angola. The Integrated Business Support Centre thus represents an important step towards the modernisation of public services and the creation of a more dynamic, efficient and competitive business ecosystem.

Hao Duo Bao — Camama Popular Shopping

The arrival of Hao Duo Bao marks a new chapter for the Camama Popular Shopping, bringing to the public a simple, accessible and varied shopping proposition. The new store stands out by offering all its products at the single price of 1,000 kwanzas, a proposal designed to attract consumers looking for quality, practicality and affordable prices in their daily lives.

Hao Duo Bao emerges as a practical, modern and fun alternative, investing in a dynamic and accessible shopping experience, where every customer can find useful and interesting products at a single price. This initiative further helps to energise the shopping centre's commercial environment, strengthening it as one of the main points of commerce and social interaction in the region.
